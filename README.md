## Privacy Policy for CrainteAI
Last updated: November 11, 2026

### CrainteAI ("we", "our", or "the extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy details our practices concerning data collection, handling, storage, and transfer when using the CrainteAI Chrome Extension.

### 1. Data Collection
CrainteAI does not collect, track, log, or sell any personally identifiable information (PII), browsing history, web traffic, or content of the web pages you visit.

### 2. Data Usage and Processing
Client-Side Execution: The extension operates locally within your browser. Page parsing, interface interactions, and prompt preparations are performed entirely on your device.

AI Analysis via Groq API: When you initiate an action requiring AI evaluation, the relevant prompt or text snippet is sent directly from your browser to Groq Inc.'s API endpoints using your personal Groq API key.

Single-Purpose Processing: Data sent to the Groq API is transmitted solely for real-time inference and generating responses inside the extension.

### 3. Data Storage and Retention
Local Storage Only: Your Groq API key and local preferences are stored exclusively on your device using Chrome's secure storage API (chrome.storage.local).

No Developer Servers: We do not operate remote databases, intermediary servers, or telemetry tracking for this extension.

Data Deletion: You retain complete control over your data. Removing your API key in the extension settings or uninstalling CrainteAI immediately and permanently purges all locally saved data.

### 4. Data Sharing and Third-Party Disclosure
We do not sell, rent, trade, or transfer your data to third-party brokers, advertisers, or analytics platforms.

Third-Party Service Provider (Groq): Inference requests are processed directly by Groq, Inc. Data transmission between your browser and Groq is governed by the Groq Privacy Policy.

No Intermediary Proxies: CrainteAI does not route your requests, keys, or queries through any proxy server operated by us or any other third party.

### 5. Security Measures
Your API key is kept locally in your browser and is transmitted exclusively over encrypted HTTPS connections directly to Groq's official API servers during active requests.

### 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect technical or legal changes. Revisions will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
