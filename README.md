# Privacy-Policy-CrainteAI
CrainteAI is built with privacy at its core.
We do not collect, store, or share any personal data from users.

All analysis happens locally in your browser — no browsing history, API keys, or personal information are ever sent to external servers.
Your API keys are saved securely on your own device and are never transmitted or visible to anyone else.

CrainteAI operates entirely client‑side, ensuring that your online activity remains private and under your control.
